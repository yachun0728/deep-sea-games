Original 2D mobile game character design sheet. Style: cheeky, playful hand-drawn comic cartoon, thick wobbly dark-plum outlines, flat bright colors with simple cel shading, exaggerated squash-and-stretch expressions. Night palette with warm punchy accents. Mood: "this mosquito is SO annoying and loud". No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos. Plain cream background, neatly spaced.
TOP HALF - the mosquito hero: a tiny round brat of a mosquito, huge smug half-lidded eyes, cheeky grin with one little fang, long needle proboscis, thin striped legs, two buzzing translucent wings, striped belly. Side view facing right. Poses: (1) smug flying with zigzag buzz lines and vibration marks, (2) taunting with tongue out, (3) landed and sucking, eyes blissfully closed, (4) very full: belly a giant glossy cherry-red balloon, sweating, heavy flight, (5) dizzy with spinning stars after a bump, (6) panicked fleeing.
BOTTOM HALF - the sleeper: a young man with spiky dark brown hair and striped pajamas, head on pillow, four annoyance stages: (a) peaceful sleep with snot bubble, (b) one eyebrow twitching, mouth scrunched, (c) gritted teeth, anger veins, pillow squeezed over his ears, dark under-eye circles, (d) totally snapped: bloodshot eyes wide open, wild hair, holding a yellow electric mosquito swatter, with scribble rage cloud. Gag style, no gore.
