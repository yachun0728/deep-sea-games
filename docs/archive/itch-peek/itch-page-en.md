## Description
You are walking through a forest at night. Things are following you. You can't see them coming. You can only hear them.

**Listen.** Every creature announces itself with a different sound, from a different side.
**Hide.** Swipe sideways to duck into a tree or a coffin. Some ghosts want you to hide. Others want you to hold the right item.
**Don't look.** Some things only hurt you if you see them. When the Bellkeeper tolls, keep your head down.

Tap the right item (lantern, talisman, hammer, bucket) when a ghost walks up. Pick up torches and charms along the road. Sit by the campfire and choose what to carry.

- 100 stages across forest, graveyard, snow and lava worlds
- 18 ways to die, and a ghost codex that fills up as you meet them
- Free run mode and a daily challenge seed
- Hand-drawn dark fantasy art

Best played with headphones. Contains horror themes, sudden loud sounds and brief flashes. A "low scare" option is available in the game.

## Controls
- Swipe left / right (or drag with the mouse): hide in a tree or coffin
- Tap / click: pick up an item, choose an item, leave a hiding spot
- Swipe down: keep your head down (Bellkeeper)
