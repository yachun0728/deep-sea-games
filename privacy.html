隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

本政策說明 TICATICA GAMES（以下稱「我們」）在網站 ticaticagames.com 與 Telegram Bot、Mini App（以下合稱「本服務」）中，會處理哪些資料、為什麼處理，以及你可以怎麼要求刪除。本服務不需要註冊帳號，也不會要求你提供姓名、電話、信箱或付款資料。

1. 存在你裝置上的資料

點數餘額、各遊戲最佳紀錄、語言設定、造型與遊戲進度存在瀏覽器的本機儲存空間（localStorage），不會上傳給我們。清除瀏覽器網站資料就會一併刪除。點數是遊戲內的虛擬點數，沒有金錢價值。

2. 訪客排行榜與分享連結

  • 第一次玩有排行榜的遊戲時，系統會建立一組隨機的訪客編號與憑證，憑證存在你的瀏覽器；伺服器只保存憑證的雜湊值。
  • 為了驗證成績，伺服器會保存該局的遊戲、操作紀錄、分數與時間。
  • 公開排行榜會顯示顯示名稱（預設為「訪客」）、遊戲、分數與上榜時間。
  • 你按下「分享成績」時，伺服器會保存遊戲、分數與語言，產生一條任何人都能開啟的連結；成績卡圖片在你的裝置上產生，不會上傳。
  • 我們不會在排行榜或分享資料中保存你的 IP 位址。

3. 流量分析（Google Analytics 4）

我們使用 Google Analytics 4 了解網站的使用情況，例如瀏覽的頁面、開始與結束遊戲、有效遊玩時間與分數、裝置與瀏覽器類型、大略地區，以及你從哪個連結進來（包含連結上的 UTM 參數）。我們已關閉 Google 信號與廣告個人化信號，也不會把姓名或聯絡方式送進 Google Analytics。Google 處理這些資料的方式見 Google 隱私權政策。

4. 廣告（Google AdSense）

網站可能顯示 Google AdSense 廣告。Google 與其合作夥伴可能使用 Cookie，依據你造訪本網站或其他網站的紀錄顯示廣告。你可以到 Google 廣告設定 關閉個人化廣告，詳見 Google 如何使用廣告 Cookie。

5. Telegram Bot 與 Mini App

  • 你傳指令給 Bot 時，Telegram 會把訊息內容、你的 Telegram 使用者編號、聊天室編號與語言設定傳給我們，用來決定要回覆的語言與按鈕。Bot 不會儲存這些資料，也不會記錄你的訊息。
  • Bot 的按鈕連結會帶上語言與來源參數，讓 Google Analytics 知道流量來自 Telegram，但不包含你的 Telegram 帳號資料。
  • 在 Telegram 內開啟的 Mini App 就是本網站，適用本政策第 1 到 4 點；我們不讀取你的 Telegram 帳號資料。
  • Telegram 本身處理資料的方式見 Telegram 隱私權政策。

6. 服務供應商

本服務架設在 Cloudflare，資料庫使用 Cloudflare D1。Cloudflare 為了安全與營運，可能短暫保存連線紀錄（例如 IP 位址）。分析與廣告由 Google 提供。這些供應商可能在你所在國家以外的地方處理資料。

7. 保存期間

  • 本機資料：直到你清除瀏覽器網站資料。
  • 排行榜紀錄與分享連結：在本服務營運期間保存，或直到你要求刪除。排行榜每款遊戲只公開前 50 名。
  • Google Analytics：依我們在 Google Analytics 設定的資料保留期限自動刪除。

8. 你的權利

你可以要求查詢或刪除與你的訪客編號相關的排行榜紀錄與分享連結。因為我們不知道你是誰，請在信中提供分享連結或你在排行榜上的名稱、遊戲與大約時間，方便我們找到資料。

9. 兒童

本服務適合一般大眾，不刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。未滿 13 歲（或你所在地區規定的年齡）的使用者，請在家長或監護人同意下使用。

10. 政策更新

我們可能更新本政策，並在本頁更新生效日期。重大變更會在網站上公告。

11. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題或要求，請寫信到 support@ticaticagames.com。

4. 广告（Google AdSense）

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what data TICATICA GAMES ("we") processes on ticaticagames.com and in our Telegram bot and Mini App (together, the "Service"), why we process it, and how you can ask us to delete it. The Service does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, phone number, email address or payment details.

1. Data stored on your device

Your point balance, best scores, language setting, outfits and game progress are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and are not uploaded to us. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes them. Points are virtual in-game points with no monetary value.

2. Guest leaderboards and share links

  • The first time you play a game with a leaderboard, the Service creates a random guest ID and credential. The credential stays in your browser; our server keeps only a hash of it.
  • To verify scores, the server stores the game, the moves you made, the score and the time of each ranked round.
  • Public leaderboards show a display name (by default "Guest"), the game, the score and the time it was recorded.
  • When you tap "Share result", the server stores the game, score and language and creates a link that anyone can open. The result card image is made on your device and is not uploaded.
  • We do not store your IP address with leaderboard or share data.

3. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, games started and finished, active play time and scores, device and browser type, approximate region, and the link that brought you here (including UTM parameters on that link). Google signals and ad personalization signals are turned off, and we never send names or contact details to Google Analytics. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site may show Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses advertising cookies.

5. Telegram bot and Mini App

  • When you send a command to the bot, Telegram passes us the message, your Telegram user ID, the chat ID and your language setting. We use them only to choose the reply language and buttons. The bot does not store this data or keep a log of your messages.
  • Links in the bot's buttons carry language and source parameters so Google Analytics can tell the visit came from Telegram. They do not include your Telegram account details.
  • The Mini App you open inside Telegram is this website, so sections 1 to 4 apply. We do not read your Telegram account data.
  • For how Telegram itself handles data, see the Telegram Privacy Policy.

6. Service providers

The Service runs on Cloudflare, and its database is Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare may briefly keep connection logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations. Analytics and ads are provided by Google. These providers may process data outside your country.

7. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you clear the site data in your browser.
  • Leaderboard records and share links: while the Service operates, or until you ask us to delete them. Each game's public leaderboard shows only the top 50.
  • Google Analytics: deleted automatically under the retention period set in our Google Analytics account.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the leaderboard records and share links tied to your guest ID. Because we do not know who you are, please include a share link, or your leaderboard name, the game and the approximate time, so we can find the data.

9. Children

The Service is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Users under 13 (or the age required where they live) should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the effective date on this page. Significant changes will be announced on the site.

11. Contact us

For questions or requests about this policy, email support@ticaticagames.com.
