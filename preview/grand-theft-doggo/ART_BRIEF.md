# Grand Theft Doggo: Vice Paw 美術提案（待使用者確認，尚未採納）

畫風（2026-11-24 使用者選定）：粗輪廓美式復古卡通角色 + 邁阿密夕陽場景。
避免：寫實毛髮、他人 IP（不要 GTA 角色與商標、不要任何真實品牌）、血腥。

## 1. 角色設定集（給 GPT 繪圖）

Prompt：
> Character design sheet of a muscular American bulldog hero for a mobile top-down-ish 3/4 action game. Retro Saturday-morning American cartoon style, thick black outlines, flat saturated colors with simple two-tone cel shading, exaggerated chunky proportions (big head, wide shoulders, short legs). Tan fur, cream muzzle, floppy dark-brown ears. Outfit: white tank top with a stars-and-stripes pattern, backwards red trucker cap with a star, gold aviator sunglasses, gold chain, denim shorts, red-white sneakers. Holds a blue tennis-ball blaster. Show: back 3/4 view (main gameplay angle, camera behind and above at 55 degrees), front view, side view, and 4 action poses (run, paw punch, dodge roll, crouch behind cover). Color palette strip: red #D62839, navy #1D3F8A, cream #F5F1E6, fur #C98A4B, gold #FFC83D. Plain light background, no text, no logos.

## 2. 敵人：貓咪黑幫（同一畫風）

> Enemy design sheet: cat mafia gang for the same cartoon style. Three variants: tuxedo cat with fedora and tennis-ball tommy gun, fat gray cat boss with pinstripe suit and cigar-free cigar-shaped chew toy, sneaky black cat with sunglasses and baseball bat. Thick outlines, flat colors, same palette family, front and back views.

## 3. 場景設定集

> Environment concept sheet for "Vice Paw Boulevard", a fictional sunny-sunset Miami-style beach city for a mobile action game, American retro cartoon style matching the dog hero. Pink-purple-orange sunset sky, palm trees, art-deco pastel buildings with neon signs (invented names only, no real brands), striped awnings, a pink convertible and a teal muscle car, hot dog stand, beach boardwalk, cracked asphalt with white dashes. Show three layers: far (skyline and sun), mid (buildings and palms), near (street, curb, props). Include a palette strip and 4 material callouts (stucco, asphalt, chrome, neon). No text, no real logos.

## 4. 手機直式遊玩構圖（390×844）

> Vertical 390x844 mobile game screenshot mockup, same cartoon style. High-angle 55-degree behind-the-shoulder view of the bulldog hero standing in the lower-right area of the screen on a sunset Miami street, three cat mafia enemies ahead, a pink convertible on the right, golden bones on the ground. Overlay clean game UI: top-left health bar and weapon label, top-center round radar, top-right cash counter and 5 bone wanted stars, left-bottom virtual joystick, right-bottom large round FIRE button with smaller DODGE, CROUCH and CAR buttons in a thumb arc, a small back arrow top-left.
